📜 INTRODUCTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pratapgad — the "Fort of Valour" — is forever etched in history as the site of the legendary encounter between Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Afzal Khan on 10 November 1659. Built by Shivaji himself in 1656, this fort witnessed one of the most dramatic episodes in Indian history — Shivaji's cunning survival and victory over the physically massive Bijapur general. A grand statue of Shivaji Maharaj stands at the entrance, and the Afzal Tower marks where the general fell.

📅 HISTORICAL TIMELINE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ┌─── 1656 ───┐
  │ Fort Construction Ordered
  │ Shivaji Maharaj ordered the construction of Pratapgad to strengthen control over the strategic pass between Javali and the Konkan coast.
  └────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─── 10 Nov 1659 ───┐
  │ ⚔️ The Fateful Encounter
  │ Shivaji met Afzal Khan at the base for "negotiations." When Afzal Khan attacked treacherously, Shivaji fought back with his wagh nakh (tiger claws) and killed him.
  └────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─── 1659 ───┐
  │ Battle of Pratapgad
  │ After Afzal Khan's death, the hidden Maratha forces descended on the Bijapur army. 3,000 Bijapur soldiers were killed, and 65 officers captured.
  └────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─── 1818 ───┐
  │ Treaty with the British
  │ The Maratha chiefs surrendered the fort to the British under the terms of the Treaty of Poona.
  └────────────────────────────────────────┘

  ┌─── 1957 ───┐
  │ Shivaji Statue Unveiled
  │ A grand equestrian statue of Shivaji Maharaj was unveiled at the fort entrance by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru.
  └────────────────────────────────────────┘

🏛️ KEY LANDMARKS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Shivaji Maharaj Statue ⭐ MUST SEE
     A striking equestrian bronze statue of Shivaji Maharaj at the fort entrance. Unveiled in 1957 by PM Nehru. Iconic photo spot.

  2. Afzal Buruj (Afzal Tower) ⭐ MUST SEE
     The tower where Afzal Khan's body was buried after the encounter. The exact spot of one of history's most dramatic events.

  3. Bhavani Temple ⭐ MUST SEE
     The sacred temple where Shivaji Maharaj worshipped Goddess Bhavani before the fateful encounter with Afzal Khan. Deeply revered.

  4. Upper Fort (Bale Killa) ⭐ MUST SEE
     The inner citadel with royal quarters, water cisterns, and watchtowers. Offers panoramic views of the Mahabaleshwar plateau.

  5. Point of Encounter
     A monument marking the exact spot below the fort where Shivaji Maharaj met Afzal Khan. Accessible via steps from the main road.

  6. Deep Mala (Lamp Tower)
     An ancient stone lamp tower used to signal other forts in the chain. A beautiful piece of Maratha architecture.

🥾 TREK ROUTES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  🚗 Via Road (Main Access)
     Difficulty: Easy | Distance: 0.5 km from parking | Time: 10-15 min
     A road goes nearly to the fort entrance. Short walk up steps from the parking area. Vehicle-accessible. Easiest of all major forts.

  🥾 Via Forest Trail (Scenic)
     Difficulty: Medium | Distance: 3 km | Time: 60-90 min
     A forest trail from Par village leading to the fort through dense jungle. More adventure, fewer crowds.

🚌 HOW TO REACH
════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  🚌 By Bus
     ST buses from Satara or Mahabaleshwar to Pratapgad. Mahabaleshwar is 24 km away. Satara is 64 km. Regular service.

  🚗 By Car
     Drive from Pune via NH48 to Satara (110 km), then to Mahabaleshwar road. OR from Mahabaleshwar (24 km). Parking at fort base.

  🏔️ From Mahabaleshwar
     Most visitors combine with Mahabaleshwar trip. Shared jeeps and taxis available from Mahabaleshwar bus stand. ₹100-200.
